The journey · 2017—2024

Three albums.
Three eras.

2017

Dua Lipa

Self-titled debut. New Rules became a global anthem with its iconic music video. IDGAF and Be the One established her as pop's new voice.

Debut era
2020

Future Nostalgia

The album that defined the pandemic. Don't Start Now, Levitating, Physical — a disco-pop masterpiece. Grammy for Best Pop Vocal Album. 6 billion streams and counting.

Disco revival
2021

Future Nostalgia: The Moonlight Edition

Deluxe reissue with Cold Heart (Pnau remix) with Elton John. Levitating became the longest-charting song by a female artist on the Hot 100.

Record-breaking
2023

Dance the Night

Barbie soundtrack lead single. A disco bop that became the song of the summer. Grammy nomination for Best Song Written for Visual Media.

Barbie era
2024

Radical Optimism

Third album. Psychedelic pop meets Britpop. Houdini and Training Season became instant hits. A more mature, confident sound.

Psychedelic pop
