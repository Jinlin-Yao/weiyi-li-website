Four albums.
Four eras.

2018

Amala

Debut album. So High and Go To Town established her quirky, genre-blending style. Largely overlooked at first, but a cult favorite.

Debut era
2019

Hot Pink

Breakthrough. Say So went viral on TikTok and became her first #1 hit. Juicy and Rules showcased her versatility. A pop star was born.

Breakthrough
2021

Planet Her

Commercial peak. Kiss Me More with SZA won a Grammy. Woman, Need to Know, Get Into It (Yuh) — every track a potential single. 3 Grammy nominations.

Grammy winner
2023

Scarlet

A radical departure. Darker, more hip-hop focused. Paint the Town Red spent 3 weeks at #1. Agora Hills and Demons showed her range. Shaved head, blood red — a new era.

Experimental era
2024

The Scarlet Tour

First arena headlining tour. A theatrical, horror-inspired stage show. Ice Spice and Doechii opened. Sold-out shows across North America and Europe.

Fourth album · 2023

Scarlet.

A rejection of pop stardom. Doja Cat raps more than she sings on Scarlet, delivering some of her most personal and aggressive verses. Paint the Town Red became her biggest solo hit. Agora Hills showed her softer side. An album that defies categorization.
